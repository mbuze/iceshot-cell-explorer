How the simulation works

ICeShOT uses optimal transport to divide the sheet among 49 cells. Each cell has a target area. Changing the gold cell’s preference stretches it into a spindle or draws out branches. Its neighbours share the remaining space. The viewer switches between five saved runs, all starting from the same tissue. The prescribed path lets us compare the effect of shape on the neighbours.
